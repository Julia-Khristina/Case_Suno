Título: Brent hits $110 on Middle East tensions, Opec+ supply cuts
Fonte: Reuters
URL: https://www.reuters.com/markets/oil/brent-hits-110
Data: 2026-10-15
Conteúdo:
Oil prices rose on Monday, with Brent crude futures climbing above $110 a barrel for the first time since 2014, driven by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and expectations of supply cuts from Opec+.

Brent futures settled at $110.45 a barrel, up 2.3%, while US West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude fell 0.5% to $107.20, reflecting concerns about the US economy.

Chinese demand also showed signs of recovery, supporting prices, traders said.
